\begin{abstract}
Consumer smartphones operating as autonomous athletic tracking instruments face severe physical and systems-level constraints: urban multipath Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) spikes, non-Gaussian horizontal accuracy reporting, intermittent signal blackouts, uncalibrated micro-electro-mechanical systems (MEMS) inertial drift, and operating-system privacy restrictions on cellular and acoustic telemetry. While academic literature proposes specialized algorithms for each sub-problem, naive adoption in a production mobile application frequently degrades real-world accuracy due to mismatched sensor semantics, lookahead assumptions, or temporal data leakage during evaluation. This paper presents a comprehensive literature review and reproducible benchmark audit covering all nine algorithmic pillars deployed in the \emph{Aetron} mobile fitness platform: (1) Phase~1 kinematic trajectory preprocessing and Cauchy M-estimator quality gating; (2) Phase~2 Normalized Innovation Squared ($\chi^2$) Mahalanobis gating and 4-state Adaptive Extended Kalman Filtering (AEKF) contrasted against historical Fixed-Alpha smoothing; (3) Phase~3 monotone Piecewise Cubic Hermite Interpolating Polynomial (PCHIP) trajectory reconstruction; (4) temporal density-based dwell clustering (T-DBSCAN), causal Auto-Pause, and rhythmic step-bout gating; (5) causal smartphone accelerometry step and cadence detection for walking and running; (6) Leave-One-Subject-Out (LOSO) biomechanical step-length estimation and missing-cadence fallbacks; (7) deep multivariate Indoor--Outdoor Detection (DeepIOD) and probabilistic carrier-to-noise ($C/N_0$) transition handover; (8) standardized metabolic energy expenditure via the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities; and (9) server-authority raw segment auditing, map-matching quality contracts, and lossy geometric polyline compression. Crucially, we dissect \emph{how each source publication derives its benchmark metrics}---spanning reference ground-truth instrumentation, holdout partitioning, and error formulations---and execute bit-reproducible comparative evaluations on Aetron's production Dart and TypeScript services across eight public datasets ($N = 2{,}694$ TU~Wien phase-RTK smartphone epochs, $20{,}770$ Oxford SnapperGPS cycling epochs, $122{,}808$ STAGA stop/trip epochs, $12$ Oxford Java traces, $39\text{ h}$ OxWalk free-living, $90$ PedEval sessions, $198$ SLEDataset2/RBDS gait trials, and $201{,}731$ held-out Cambridge CIOD epochs), explicitly preserving negative results and sensor-privilege data gaps.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Architectural Context}
% =============================================================================

Modern mobile exercise applications must simultaneously deliver sub-second live telemetry on resource-constrained smartphones (iOS and Android) and auditable post-workout metrics in the cloud. Unlike survey-grade geodetic receivers or foot-mounted inertial navigation units (employing zero-velocity updates, ZUPTs), a handheld or pocket-carried smartphone provides asynchronous, noisy observations: 1~Hz GNSS fixes subject to non-line-of-sight (NLOS) urban canyon reflections, uncalibrated 3-axis MEMS accelerometry corrupted by arm swing and resting hand tremor, and restricted environmental sensors.

In the \emph{Aetron} platform, runtime execution is partitioned across an offline-first Flutter mobile client (persisting raw GPS fixes, step intervals, and 5-second crash-recovery checkpoints into an ACID-durable Isar database alongside a relational SQLite profile store) and deterministic Deno edge workers backed by PostgreSQL/PostGIS. To prevent silent metric corruption across these boundaries, Aetron enforces four foundational scientific governance rules:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Strict Provenance Separation:} Published reference equations and thresholds are distinguished from Aetron-fitted parameters and engineering safety gates; author-reported paper numbers are never conflated with locally rerun benchmark outputs.
  \item \textbf{Task-Consistent Baselines:} Historical coordinate smoothers (specifically Aetron's legacy \texttt{Fixed Alpha} filter) are compared strictly against positioning filters on shared UTC-matched trajectories using Root Mean Square Error (RMSE), Mean Absolute Error (MAE), and contiguous distance error---never conflated with binary \textsc{Stop}/\textsc{Trip} classification F1 scores.
  \item \textbf{Causal Real-Time Execution:} Offline algorithms requiring future lookahead windows (e.g., bidirectional Stop \& Go segmentation or spline gap bridging) are barred from mutating live workout distance or real-time 3-second Auto-Pause triggers.
  \item \textbf{Zero Data Leakage and Honest Negative Reporting:} Subject-grouped (LOSO) and session-grouped holdouts are enforced across all learned models; negative results on out-of-domain hardware are preserved verbatim rather than masked by test-set tuning.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Pillar 1: GNSS Trajectory Preprocessing and Robust Quality Gating (P01)}
Raw consumer GNSS streams exhibit non-chronological callbacks, acute single-epoch multipath spikes, stationary scatter, and tunnel blackouts. Zheng \cite{zheng2015trajectory} and Li \emph{et al.} \cite{li2025trajectory} formalize trajectory cleaning as sequential constraint verification over time-ordered fixes $p_k = (\phi_k, \lambda_k, t_k, \sigma_k)$. First, Aetron's \texttt{GpsRawSignalContinuity} enforces strict monotonicity ($\Delta t_k = t_k - t_{k-1} > 0$) and partitions temporal blackouts whenever $\Delta t_k > \Delta t_{\text{gap}} = 5.0\text{ s}$. Across an outage gap, distance accumulation is suspended and the downstream filter anchor is reset rather than fabricating chord distance through buildings or tunnels.

Second, modality-specific physical envelopes $\mathcal{L}_m = (v_{\max}^{(m)}, a_{\max}^{(m)}, \sigma_{\max}^{(m)}, d_{\min}^{(m)})$ are enforced in \texttt{KinematicLimits}:
\begin{equation}
  v_k = \frac{d_{\text{geo}}(p_{k-1}, p_k)}{\Delta t_k} \le v_{\max}^{(m)}, \qquad
  a_k = \frac{|v_k - v_{k-1}|}{\Delta t_k} \le a_{\max}^{(m)},
  \label{eq:p01_kinematic}
\end{equation}
parameterized from biomechanical ceilings as $(3.5\text{ m/s}, 2.5\text{ m/s}^2, 25\text{ m}, 0.8\text{ m})$ for walking, $(8.0\text{ m/s}, 4.5\text{ m/s}^2, 25\text{ m}, 1.2\text{ m})$ for running, and $(22.0\text{ m/s}, 6.0\text{ m/s}^2, 30\text{ m}, 2.5\text{ m})$ for cycling, alongside acute ping-pong bearing-reversal rejection ($\Delta\psi_k > 140^\circ$, or $130^\circ$ for cycling) and batch sliding-window median filtering ($W = 5$) \cite{zheng2015trajectory,li2025trajectory}. For offline segmentation, Zheng \emph{et al.}'s stay-point algorithm \cite{zheng2009mining} identifies maximal sub-sequences $\{p_i,\dots,p_j\}$ satisfying $d_{\text{geo}}(p_i, p_r) \le D_{\max}^{(m)}$ for all $i \le r \le j$ and $t_j - t_i \ge T_{\min} = 10.0\text{ s}$, collapsing stationary dwells to their centroid with zero movement distance.

Because 1~Hz GNSS white position noise ($\sigma \approx 2.5\text{--}5\text{ m}$) induces apparent finite-difference acceleration spikes of $5\text{--}8\text{ m/s}^2$ that cause rigid threshold gates to drop valid fixes, Aetron additionally implements a feature-flagged robust M-estimator gate inspired by Huber \cite{huber1981robust} and Song \emph{et al.} (Mobile-GVIO) \cite{song2025mobilegvio}. Given normalized innovation $r_k = \|\mathbf{p}_k - \hat{\mathbf{p}}_{k|k-1}\| / \max(2.5, \sigma_k)$ with nominal and critical multipliers $k_1 = 2.0, k_2 = 5.0$, moderate spikes ($k_1 < r_k \le k_2$ or $a_k > a_{\max}^{(m)}$) are retained with Cauchy-inflated measurement variance scale:
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    w_k &= \frac{1}{1 + \max(0, r_k - k_1)^2}, \\
    S_{R,k} &= \mathrm{clamp}\!\left(\frac{1}{w_k}\max\!\left(1, \min\!\left(3, \frac{a_k}{a_{\max}^{(m)}}\right)\right), 1.5, 9.0\right).
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:gvio_cauchy}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Pillar 2: Adaptive GNSS Error Correction and 4-State EKF vs.\ Fixed Alpha (P02)}
Historically, Aetron smoothed accepted coordinates via an exponential moving-average filter (\texttt{Fixed Alpha}) with confidence-tiered gains $\alpha \in \{0.34, 0.52, 0.72\}$ boosted to $\alpha = 0.82$ when bearing deflection exceeded $28^\circ$ over displacements $>4\text{ m}$:
\begin{equation}
  \tilde{\mathbf{p}}_k = (1 - \alpha_k)\tilde{\mathbf{p}}_{k-1} + \alpha_k \mathbf{p}_k.
  \label{eq:fixed_alpha}
\end{equation}
While computationally trivial, (\ref{eq:fixed_alpha}) lacks kinematic velocity-heading coupling and suffers phase lag on curves. Production Aetron replaces (\ref{eq:fixed_alpha}) with a 4-state Extended Kalman Filter (\texttt{ExtendedKalmanFilter}) in local equirectangular East--North--Up (ENU) meters $\mathbf{x}_k = [p_E, p_N, v, \theta]^\top$ ($\theta$ clockwise from North):
\begin{equation}
  f(\mathbf{x}_{k-1}, \Delta t) =
  \begin{bmatrix}
    p_E + v \sin\theta \, \Delta t \\
    p_N + v \cos\theta \, \Delta t \\
    v \\
    \mathrm{wrap}_{[0,2\pi)}(\theta)
  \end{bmatrix}, \qquad
  \mathbf{F}_k = \frac{\partial f}{\partial \mathbf{x}}\bigg|_{\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k-1|k-1}}.
  \label{eq:ekf_predict}
\end{equation}

To harden the EKF against urban multipath without discarding genuine maneuvers, Phase~2 (\texttt{AdaptiveGnssCorrectionEngine}) synthesizes three techniques from Bar-Shalom \emph{et al.} \cite{barshalom2001estimation}, Groves \cite{groves2013principles}, Rong Li \& Jilkov \cite{rongli2003survey}, and Sukhenko \emph{et al.} \cite{sukhenko2025gnss}:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{2-DOF Mahalanobis Innovation Gating:} Forming the $2\times 2$ spatial innovation covariance $\mathbf{S}_k = \mathbf{H}\mathbf{P}_{k|k-1}\mathbf{H}^\top + \mathbf{R}_k$ with $\mathbf{R}_k = \max(2.25, \sigma_k^2)\mathbf{I}_2$ and Cauchy--Schwarz off-diagonal clamping $|P_{EN}| \le 0.99\sqrt{P_{EE}P_{NN}}$ (guaranteeing $\det(\mathbf{S}_k) \ge 5.0625 > 0$), the exact quadratic Normalized Innovation Squared (NIS) statistic is:
  \begin{equation}
    d_M^2 = \mathbf{y}_k^\top \mathbf{S}_k^{-1} \mathbf{y}_k = \frac{y_E^2 S_{NN} - 2 y_E y_N S_{EN} + y_N^2 S_{EE}}{S_{EE}S_{NN} - S_{EN}^2}.
    \label{eq:nis}
  \end{equation}
  Against $\chi^2_2$ quantiles $\gamma_1 = 5.991$ ($95\%$) and $\gamma_2 = 13.816$ ($99.9\%$), fixes with $d_M^2 \le \gamma_1$ update nominally; fixes with $\gamma_1 < d_M^2 \le \gamma_2$ undergo adaptive measurement standard-deviation inflation $\sigma_{\text{eff}} = \sigma_k\sqrt{d_M^2 / \gamma_1}$ \cite{groves2013principles}; and severe multipath jumps ($d_M^2 > \gamma_2$) are rejected unless $N_{\text{reanchor}} = 4$ consecutive critical innovations confirm a genuine sudden trajectory shift.
  \item \textbf{Low-Speed Doppler Heading Deadband:} Below $v_{\text{dead}} \in \{0.8, 1.2, 1.8\}\text{ m/s}$ (walk/run/cycle), noisy GNSS course measurements are suppressed before state update.
  \item \textbf{Centripetal Turn-Rate Process Noise Scaling:} When circular heading rate $\dot{\psi}_k = |\Delta\psi_k|/\Delta t_k$ exceeds $\omega_{\text{th}} \in \{20^\circ, 15^\circ, 10^\circ\}\text{/s}$, heading process variance is scaled by $\mu_\theta = \min(\mu_{\max}, 1 + 0.8(\dot{\psi}_k/\omega_{\text{th}})^2)$ \cite{rongli2003survey}, preventing filter overshoot on track bends while damping arm-swing jitter.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Pillar 3: Monotone PCHIP Trajectory Reconstruction (P03)}
Consumer wearables and smartphones frequently drop $2\text{--}15\text{ s}$ of fixes under tree canopy or overpasses. Czerwi{\'n}ski \emph{et al.} \cite{czerwinski2026reconstruction} propose multi-stage offline trajectory reconstruction, while Fritsch and Carlson \cite{fritsch1980monotone} establish necessary and sufficient conditions for monotone $C^1$ cubic Hermite interpolation (PCHIP). Given accepted boundary states $(\mathbf{p}_0, \mathbf{v}_0)$ and $(\mathbf{p}_1, \mathbf{v}_1)$ across a recoverable gap $2.0\text{ s} < \Delta t \le 15.0\text{ s}$ with secant velocity $\boldsymbol{\Delta} = (\mathbf{p}_1 - \mathbf{p}_0)/\Delta t$, each coordinate's endpoint slope ratios $\alpha = \max(0, v_0/\Delta), \beta = \max(0, v_1/\Delta)$ are projected onto the monotonicity disk $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 \le 9$:
\begin{equation}
  (\tilde{v}_0, \tilde{v}_1) =
  \begin{cases}
    (0, 0), & |\Delta \cdot \Delta t| < 10^{-4}\text{ m}, \\
    (\tau\alpha\Delta, \tau\beta\Delta), & \alpha^2 + \beta^2 > 9,\; \tau = \frac{3}{\sqrt{\alpha^2+\beta^2}}, \\
    (\alpha\Delta, \beta\Delta), & \text{otherwise}.
  \end{cases}
  \label{eq:pchip}
\end{equation}
Intermediate visual coordinates at $s = (t - t_0)/\Delta t \in (0,1)$ are evaluated on the cubic Hermite basis. Gaps exceeding $15.0\text{ s}$ are isolated as \texttt{indeterminateOutage} without synthesis. Crucially, Aetron restricts P03 strictly to offline map display; reconstructed points never enter live pace, saved distance, or server segment audits.

\subsection{Pillar 4: Spatial--Temporal Dwell Clustering (T-DBSCAN), Auto-Pause, and Step Motion Gating}
Adapting Ester \emph{et al.}'s DBSCAN \cite{ester1996dbscan} to time-ordered trajectories, \texttt{GpsDbscanService} scans sequential stationary candidates ($v_k \le 1.0\text{ m/s}$) and expands a dwell cluster while $d_{\text{geo}}(\bar{\mathbf{c}}_{i:j-1}, p_j) \le \varepsilon = 6.5\text{ m}$, confirming a stop when point count $\ge \mathrm{MinPts} = 4$ and duration $t_e - t_s \ge 10.0\text{ s}$. Complementarily, in live recording, \texttt{StepMotionGate} eliminates non-locomotor phone handling by requiring a rhythmic bout of $k_{\min} = 5$ consecutive steps (inter-step gap $\le 2.2\text{ s}$, active timeout $3.5\text{ s}$) and enforcing an outdoor stationary GNSS lock: if high-accuracy satellite lock proves zero spatial displacement beyond a $3.5\text{ s}$ onset grace window, buffered in-place impulses are purged.

\subsection{Pillar 5: Causal Inertial Step and Cadence Detection}
\subsubsection{Walking (Salvi \emph{et al.}, EMBC 2018)}
\texttt{OxfordStepDetector} adapts the Oxford Windowed Peak Detection (WPD) pipeline \cite{salvi2018oxford}. Raw 3-axis acceleration magnitude $\|\mathbf{a}\| = \sqrt{a_x^2 + a_y^2 + a_z^2}$ (including gravity) is linearly interpolated onto a uniform $100\text{ Hz}$ grid ($\Delta t = 10\text{ ms}$), convolved with a 13-tap Gaussian FIR kernel ($w_i \propto \exp[-\frac{1}{2}((i-6)/(0.35\times 6))^2]$), and scored at the midpoint of a 35-sample window via $s_k = \frac{1}{34}\sum_{j=0}^{34}(\tilde{a}_{k,17} - \tilde{a}_{k,j})$. Online Welford mean $\mu_n$ and standard deviation $\sigma_n$ gate candidate peaks via:
\begin{equation}
  s_n - \mu_n > \max(1.2\sigma_n,\, s_{\min}), \qquad
  \max_{w \in W_{100}} \|\mathbf{a}\|_w - \min_{w \in W_{100}} \|\mathbf{a}\|_w \ge R_{\min},
  \label{eq:oxford}
\end{equation}
where $s_{\min} = 0.35\text{ m/s}^2$ and 1-second dynamic span $R_{\min} = 1.40\text{ m/s}^2$ suppress resting tremor. Whereas the reference Java implementation confirms a step only when the \emph{next} separated peak arrives (dropping terminal steps), Aetron causally confirms the strongest pending peak once its $200\text{ ms}$ refractory window expires.

\subsubsection{Running (Xu \emph{et al.}, 2022) and ZUPT-GLRT Stationary Rejection}
\texttt{XuRunningStepDetector} implements a causal adaptation of Xu \emph{et al.} \cite{xu2022step}. Acceleration magnitude is interpolated onto a $50\text{ Hz}$ grid, smoothed by an 11-tap Hamming-windowed low-pass FIR filter ($f_c = 0.1 f_s = 5\text{ Hz}$) alongside a 15-sample moving average, and analyzed every $1\text{ s}$ via a 256-point real Discrete Fourier Transform ($\approx 5.12\text{ s}$ buffer) to extract dominant gait frequency $f_{\text{dom}}$. Rather than relying on ad-hoc peak-to-peak bounds vulnerable to isolated screen taps, both \texttt{OxfordStepDetector} and \texttt{XuRunningStepDetector} apply the Magnitude-Variance Zero-Velocity Generalized Likelihood Ratio Test (MV-GLRT) of Skog \emph{et al.} \cite{skog2010zupt} (Eq.~13),
\begin{equation}
  T_{\text{MV}}(n) = \frac{1}{W}\sum_{k=n-W+1}^{n} \bigl(\|\mathbf{a}_k\|_2 - \bar{a}_n\bigr)^2 < \gamma_{\text{MV}},
  \label{eq:skog_zupt}
\end{equation}
calibrated via Brajdic and Harle's unconstrained-smartphone walk-detection SD floor \cite{brajdic2013walk} ($\gamma_{\text{MV}} = 0.36\text{ (m/s}^2)^2$ for walking; $0.64\text{ (m/s}^2)^2$ for running), rejecting $100\%$ of stationary hand-sway and impulsive screen taps ($0$ false steps over $60\text{ s}$) while preserving $4{,}225/4{,}107$ steps on Oxford WPD and $1{,}265/1{,}280$ on Xu Group~3. Downstream, \texttt{StepMotionGate} enforces sustained rhythmic bout confirmation ($N_{\text{bout}} = 5$ steps within $[0.65, 4.5]\text{ Hz}$ \cite{brajdic2013walk}) coupled with an outdoor stationary GNSS safeguard.

\subsection{Pillar 6: Biomechanical Adaptive Step-Length Estimation (SLE)}
Static height-proportional stride rules ($L = k H$) systematically under-estimate fast gait and over-estimate slow gait. Aetron adopts Renaudin \emph{et al.}'s linear height--step-frequency formulation \cite{renaudin2012step} (Eq.~8):
\begin{equation}
  L_{\text{step}}(H, f) = H \cdot (a f + b) + c,
  \label{eq:renaudin}
\end{equation}
where $H$ is stature in meters and $f = \mathrm{Cadence}_{\text{spm}}/60$ is step frequency in Hz. Coefficients $(a, b, c)$ are fitted independently on public walking (\texttt{SLEDataset2}) and running (Fukuchi \emph{et al.} \cite{fukuchi2017public} RBDS) cohorts after Leave-One-Subject-Out validation: $(0.1328, 0.0217, 0.2755)$ for walking ($H \in [1.60, 1.88]\text{ m}$, $\mathrm{Cadence} \in [94, 144]\text{ spm}$) and $(0.3211, 0.7253, -1.6810)$ for running ($H \in [1.627, 1.872]\text{ m}$, $\mathrm{Cadence} \in [143, 223]\text{ spm}$). Outside validated physiological support or when live cadence is unavailable, \texttt{ResearchStepLengthFallback} substitutes cohort-mean frequency $\mathbb{E}[f]$ ($1.8836\text{ Hz}$ walk; $2.8687\text{ Hz}$ run) or population mean step length ($0.7541\text{ m}$ walk; $1.2134\text{ m}$ run) rather than extrapolating unboundedly.

\subsection{Pillar 7: Context-Aware Indoor--Outdoor Detection and Cross-Platform iOS/Android Handover}
Dastagir \emph{et al.} (DeepIOD) \cite{dastagir2024deepiod} combine three 10-second sliding-window neural architectures---Model~A (1D-CNN + LSTM), Model~B (1D-CNN + Multi-Head Attention), and Model~C (2D Depthwise-Separable CNN, DWS-Conv2D)---over 8 features (\texttt{RSRP}, \texttt{RSRQ}, \texttt{Light}, \texttt{Mag}, \texttt{Acc}, \texttt{Sound}, \texttt{Proximity}, \texttt{Daytime}) with Adaptive Majority Voting (AMV):
\begin{equation}
  \hat{C} = \arg\max_{j \in \{\text{In},\text{Out}\}} \sum_{i} w_i \, p_{i,j}, \qquad
  p_{\text{neural}} = w_A p_A + w_B p_B + w_C p_C.
  \label{eq:deepiod_amv}
\end{equation}
Because iOS prohibits public access to ambient light photodiodes, raw GNSS $C/N_0$, and Wi-Fi RSSI scans (and unprivileged mobile apps cannot read cellular PHY \texttt{RSRP}/\texttt{RSRQ}), Aetron routes platforms by verified sensor availability:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Android Path (with Ambient Light \& GNSS $C/N_0$):} Runs the 4-channel \texttt{Model C-Mobile} (\texttt{[Light, Mag, Acc, Daytime]}) alongside Yoo's probabilistic $C/N_0$ transition detector \cite{yoo2025seamless} ($30 \le C/N_0 \le 40\text{ dB-Hz}$).
  \item \textbf{iOS-First Light-Free Path (\texttt{IosMultiSensorIodClassifier}):} Extracts 10 causal 10-second magnetic perturbation features following \emph{MagIO} (Ashraf \emph{et al.}, 2018 \cite{ashraf2018magio}) coupled with linear acceleration dynamics (Esmaeili Kelishomi \emph{et al.}, 2019 \cite{kelishomi2019activities}) and astronomical daytime, fused via IODetector confidence weighting \cite{li2014iodetector} with CoreLocation accuracy/outage likelihoods (Bui \emph{et al.}, 2020 \cite{bui2020gpsiod}) and \texttt{CMAltimeter} vertical pressure transitions ($|\Delta P_{10\text{s}}| \ge 0.35\text{ hPa}$). Frame-level emissions $b_t(s)$ are smoothed by a causal 2-state Hidden Markov Model forward filter (Zhu \emph{et al.}, 2019 \cite{zhu2019fast}):
\begin{equation}
  \alpha_t(s) \propto b_t(s) \sum_{s' \in \{\text{In},\text{Out}\}} \alpha_{t-1}(s')\, A_{s',s},
  \label{eq:iod_hmm}
\end{equation}
  preventing single-epoch urban-canyon flickers while enabling $3\text{--}4\text{ s}$ transition tracking. Likewise, \texttt{EnvironmentClassifier} replaces ad-hoc linear score points with sigmoid GNSS accuracy likelihoods, Benhamou's path straightness index $S = d_{\text{net}}/\max(d_{\text{path}}, 1)$ \cite{benhamou2004tortuosity}, a GPS-blackout active-step gate, and (\ref{eq:iod_hmm}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Pillar 8: Metabolic Energy Expenditure via the 2024 Adult Compendium}
Both client (\texttt{UserProfile}) and server (\texttt{compendium\_met.ts}) compute gross kilocalories using exact 5-digit activity codes from the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities \cite{herrmann2024compendium} (codes \texttt{17151--17255} walking, \texttt{12028--12145} running, \texttt{01010--01060} cycling) converted to miles per hour ($v_{\text{mph}} = v_{\text{km/h}}/1.609344$) without unverified interpolation across speed-band gaps, gated strictly by active moving time $t_{\text{mov}}$:
\begin{equation}
  E_{\text{kcal}} = \mathrm{MET}(\text{activity}, v_{\text{mph}}) \times m_{\text{kg}} \times \frac{t_{\text{mov}}}{3600},
  \label{eq:met}
\end{equation}
returning $0\text{ kcal}$ whenever verified displacement is zero.

\subsection{Pillar 9: Server-Authority Reconciliation, Map-Matching Contracts, and Polyline Compression}
To transmit multi-hour trajectories over mobile networks, \texttt{PolylineCompressionService} implements Google's lossy delta-quantized variable-length 5-bit ASCII encoding at $10^{-5}$ ($\approx 1.1\text{ m}$ equatorial resolution) or $10^{-6}$ degrees. Upon session completion, \texttt{deterministic-finalize-worker} audits raw GPS legs against kinematic and accuracy gates ($20/50/100\text{ m}$), reconciles canonical metrics (\texttt{METRICS\_VERSION = 2}), and archives client P03 splines strictly as display artifacts. Subsequently, \texttt{route-correction-worker} partitions routes into 100-point chunks (8-point overlap) designed for Hidden Markov Model (HMM) road-network matching \cite{newson2009hidden}, awarding high-confidence distance authority ($\ge 0.90$) only when coverage $\ge 90\%$ and distance delta $\le 8\%$. When no external OSRM road-graph engine is attached (\texttt{provider = "stub"}), \texttt{routeMatchingAvailable()} explicitly returns \texttt{false}, falling back to filtered GNSS geometry rather than fabricating map-match confidence.

\section{How Source Papers Derive Their Benchmark Numbers}
% =============================================================================

A rigorous engineering reproduction requires understanding the exact experimental apparatus, temporal/subject isolation masks, and step-by-step mathematical equations behind published benchmark numbers across all evaluated domains:

\subsection{Geodetic GNSS Positioning, Athletic Distance, and Gap Reconstruction}
In smartphone GNSS studies \cite{tuwien2025dataset,sukhenko2025gnss,beuchert2023snappergps}, the test device is rigidly co-mounted with a dual-frequency carrier-phase Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) reference receiver (e.g., Leica survey prism/GNSS or u-blox ZED-F9P) providing $1\text{--}2\text{ cm}$ horizontal ground truth $\mathbf{p}_t^{\text{RTK}}$. Raw benchmark derivation proceeds in four strict steps:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{UTC Epoch Intersection:} Because OS/NMEA logger clocks exhibit sub-second jitter or systematic buffering lag (e.g., Pixel~7~Pro logger delay $\approx 2.55\text{ s}$ vs.\ GGA UTC time in \cite{tuwien2025dataset}), fixes are anchored to GNSS UTC timestamps within $\pm 500\text{ ms}$, and duplicate reference seconds are dropped.
  \item \textbf{Shared Mask Enforcement:} Competing filters reject different outlier subsets; evaluating each filter only on its own surviving fixes biases RMSE downward for aggressive gates. Every compared variant must therefore be scored on a shared \emph{common epoch mask} $\mathcal{T}_{\text{common}}$, reporting availability separately.
  \item \textbf{Pointwise Geodesic Error:} For each $t \in \mathcal{T}_{\text{common}}$, compute geodesic error $e_t = d_{\text{geo}}(\hat{\mathbf{p}}_t, \mathbf{p}_t^{\text{RTK}})$ and aggregate via RMSE, MAE, Median, and 95th percentile (P95):
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{RMSE} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{|\mathcal{T}_{\text{common}}|}\sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}_{\text{common}}} e_t^2}, \;\;
  \mathrm{MAE} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{T}_{\text{common}}|}\sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}_{\text{common}}} e_t.
  \label{eq:rmse_mae}
\end{equation}
  \item \textbf{Cumulative Distance Validity \& Masked Dropouts:} Contiguous adjacent trajectory length $D_{\text{adj}} = \sum_i d_{\text{geo}}(\hat{\mathbf{p}}_i, \hat{\mathbf{p}}_{i-1})$ is compared against RTK reference distance via $\mathrm{APE} = 100\% \times |D_{\text{adj}} - D^{\text{ref}}|/D^{\text{ref}}$. Across $K$ athletic trials, Scott \emph{et al.} \cite{scott2016validity} classify $\mathrm{MAPE} = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \mathrm{APE}_k$ as \textbf{Good} ($<5\%$), \textbf{Moderate} ($5\text{--}10\%$), or \textbf{Poor} ($>10\%$), while Gilgen-Ammann \emph{et al.} \cite{gilgenammann2020wrist} report signed percentage bias alongside Bland--Altman $95\%$ Limits of Agreement ($\bar{\Delta} \pm 1.96\,s_{\Delta}$, commercial sport watches: $0.9\text{--}4.1\%$ open track, $3.9\text{--}8.9\%$ urban). For P03 gap reconstruction \cite{czerwinski2026reconstruction}, trajectories are artificially decimated (retaining anchor fixes at $t \equiv 0 \pmod{5\text{ s}}$) and (\ref{eq:rmse_mae}) is evaluated strictly on masked interior epochs $\mathcal{M} = \{t : t \not\equiv 0 \pmod{5\text{ s}}\}$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Dwell Clustering and Real-Time Stop/Motion Classification}
Stop/trip papers \cite{zheng2009mining} collect continuous GPS traces paired with participant mobility diaries or timestamped stop logs labeling each epoch $y_t \in \{\textsc{Stop}, \textsc{Trip}\}$. From epoch-level confusion counts ($\mathrm{TP}, \mathrm{FP}, \mathrm{FN}, \mathrm{TN}$ with positive class $\textsc{Stop}$), papers report $\mathrm{Precision} = \mathrm{TP}/(\mathrm{TP}+\mathrm{FP})$, $\mathrm{Recall} = \mathrm{TP}/(\mathrm{TP}+\mathrm{FN})$, and $\mathrm{F1} = 2\,\mathrm{Precision}\cdot\mathrm{Recall}/(\mathrm{Precision}+\mathrm{Recall})$. For real-time athletic Auto-Pause, two additional operational metrics are essential: (i) \textbf{false-stop bout count} (contiguous $\mathrm{FP}$ episodes triggered during genuine slow movement) and (ii) \textbf{transition latency} $\Delta t_{\text{pause}} = t_{\text{trigger}} - t_{\text{halt}}$. Crucially, coordinate smoothers such as \texttt{Fixed Alpha} are not binary classifiers and must never be conflated with $\textsc{Stop}/\textsc{Trip}$ F1, and offline density clusterers inspecting future points cannot substitute directly for causal 3-second Auto-Pause without disclosing lookahead delay.

\subsection{Inertial Step Counting, Cadence, and Biomechanical Stride Length}
Step-counting papers \cite{salvi2018oxford,xu2022step} record $50\text{--}200\text{ Hz}$ triaxial accelerometry across multiple body placements (hand, pocket, bag, armband) synchronized against manual tally counters, $60\text{ fps}$ lower-limb video, or foot switches. Because session-total Weighted Absolute Percentage Error ($\mathrm{WAPE} = \sum_k |N_k^{\text{pred}} - N_k^{\text{ref}}| / \sum_k N_k^{\text{ref}}$) can conceal mutually cancelling false positives and false negatives, free-living evaluations (OxWalk, PedEval) slice sessions into non-overlapping $W \in \{3, 6, 10\}\text{ s}$ windows, convert step counts to $\hat{c}_w = 60\,\Delta N_w / W\text{ (spm)}$, and compute windowed cadence MAE separately over active locomotion ($c_w^{\text{ref}} > 0$) and stationary rest ($c_w^{\text{ref}} = 0$).

In stride-length studies \cite{renaudin2012step,fukuchi2017public}, step/stride truth $L_i^{\text{ref}}$ is measured via optical motion capture (Vicon) or instrumented force-plate treadmills across controlled speed bands. Fitting $(a,b,c)$ in (\ref{eq:renaudin}) and evaluating on the same subjects memorizes subject anthropometry; faithful benchmarking enforces \textbf{Leave-One-Subject-Out (LOSO)}: for each subject $s \in \{1,\dots,M\}$, fit $(a^{(-s)}, b^{(-s)}, c^{(-s)})$ via ordinary least squares exclusively on subjects $\{1,\dots,M\}\setminus\{s\}$, predict $\hat{L}_{s,i} = H_s(a^{(-s)} f_{s,i} + b^{(-s)}) + c^{(-s)}$ on held-out subject $s$, and pool cycle/session MAE ($\text{cm}$), RMSE ($\text{cm}$), and MAPE ($\%$).

\subsection{Multivariate Time-Series IOD, Compression, and Calorimetry Audits}
DeepIOD \cite{dastagir2024deepiod} and Cambridge CIOD \cite{bakirtzis2022ciod} annotate 1~Hz multi-sensor traces across indoor, outdoor, and doorway-transition segments. Because 10-second windows stepped by $1\text{--}2\text{ s}$ share $80\text{--}90\%$ of their underlying samples, random window-level train/test splits leak near-identical sensor sequences across folds and inflate accuracy above $97\%$. Moreover, skewed indoor duration ($58.97\%$ in held-out CIOD) rewards majority guessing; therefore, papers and audits must enforce \textbf{contiguous session-disjoint splits}, reset temporal hysteresis at session boundaries, and report balanced $\text{Macro-F1} = \frac{1}{2}(\mathrm{F1}_{\text{In}} + \mathrm{F1}_{\text{Out}})$ alongside doorway handover delay ($\text{s}$). Finally, polyline compression is benchmarked deterministically via byte-saving ratio $1 - B_{\text{enc}}/B_{\text{raw}}$ and round-trip geodesic quantization RMSE, while Compendium MET implementations \cite{herrmann2024compendium} are audited for exact formulaic client--server parity rather than unmeasured $\dot{V}\text{O}_2$ indirect calorimetry accuracy.

\section{Data Gaps and Methodological Boundaries}
% =============================================================================

In accordance with Aetron's thesis research policy, four explicit limitations and operational boundaries are documented:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Proxy vs.\ Native Smartphone Horizontal Accuracy:} TU~Wien NMEA logs supply $\mathrm{HDOP}\times 4$ (floored at $2.5\text{ m}$) and SnapperGPS supplies CEP50 radii without Doppler velocity, neither of which equals native iOS \texttt{CLLocation.horizontalAccuracy} or Android \texttt{Location.getAccuracy()}. Public smartphone cycling RTK datasets (e.g., SignAlign) remain unavailable upstream (empty repository shell).
  \item \textbf{Sensor Privilege Gap in Mobile IOD:} Removing privileged cellular \texttt{RSRP}/\texttt{RSRQ} and privacy-sensitive \texttt{Sound} reduces CIOD outdoor recall from $85.20\%$ (8-channel) to $45.28\%$ (4-channel) during nighttime or shaded outdoor walks where illuminance mimics indoor lighting. Consequently, DeepIOD remains strictly observational in Aetron.
  \item \textbf{Calorimetry and Stub Map-Matching Boundaries:} Compendium MET values \cite{herrmann2024compendium} represent healthy adult population averages; individual $\dot{V}\text{O}_2$ indirect calorimetry error cannot be claimed without metabolic cart trials. Likewise, when \texttt{ROUTE\_MATCH\_PROVIDER=stub}, \texttt{route-correction-worker} returns non-authoritative status rather than fabricating OSRM road-graph accuracy.
  \item \textbf{Preservation of Negative Results and Holdout Hygiene:} Ground-truth labels are never passed as runtime inputs, tuning is never performed on held-out test splits, and negative results (SnapperGPS cycling AEKF under CEP50; raw-acceleration stride divergences on mixed gait) are preserved verbatim in the benchmark record.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
% =============================================================================

By grounding every stage of mobile athletic sensing in verifiable published literature and auditing production code against public geodetic, biomechanical, and multi-sensor benchmarks, Aetron eliminates the gap between academic equations and resilient mobile software. On smartphone phase-RTK pedestrian trajectories, coupling Phase~1 kinematic preprocessing with Phase~2 Mahalanobis-gated 4-state AEKF reduces contiguous distance error from $9.77\%$ (\texttt{Fixed Alpha}) and $11.55\%$ (\texttt{Legacy EKF}) to $1.34\%$, while causal Oxford and Xu inertial detectors achieve $93.16\%$ walking and $98.69\%$ running step accuracy, LOSO-calibrated Renaudin models reduce fast-gait stride error by $4.0\text{--}5.6\text{ pp}$, and dual Isar--Supabase server reconciliation guarantees durable, scientifically auditable workout records.
